Only one backup streams to the QNAP at a time (the producer slot — the network is the bottleneck); tape writing of earlier chunks overlaps freely. “Staging” is just the chunks currently in flight on the bus — bounded per run by max_inflight_chunks and globally by qnap_budget_bytes, never the whole dataset. Each blob is freed the instant it is verified on tape.
